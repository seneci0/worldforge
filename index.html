What gold is for

The game runs in real time, so gold buys time. The cost always follows the time that is left, so an almost finished item costs very little.

  • Finish a building8 per remaining day
  • Finish training6 per remaining day
  • Finish an infrastructure upgrade8 per remaining day
  • Finish research48 per remaining day
  • Instant mobilization120 per level

You can also spend gold on resources, a country color or a premium account.

Frequently asked questions.

Ask on Discord
Is Worldforge free to play?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. Gold is optional: every player starts with 150 gold and earns more by playing, from achievements and daily rewards.

How does online play work?

You play in one persistent world with other players. Time always runs, even when you are logged out, so keep an eye on your country. Players can offer each other peace, alliances or trade; you will find offers in the Diplomacy tab.

How do I win?

You win by controlling 45% of all provinces (rebel territories don't count). A world lasts at most 2 months of real time, then the final ranking is decided by score: population, income, military strength, research and number of provinces. You don't have to fight – a rich and educated country can win through trade, coalitions and influence.

Which account do I use in the gold store?

The same player name and password you use in the game. Your profile gets its online account the first time you log in to the game with an internet connection.

When will I get the gold?

As soon as PayPal confirms the payment. If the game is running, the gold appears within a minute; otherwise the next time you log in. The game shows a notification when it arrives.

How can I pay?

Payments are processed by PayPal. You pay with your PayPal account or, where PayPal offers it, with a card. Czech visitors pay in CZK, everyone else in EUR.

What can I spend gold on?

Gold speeds up construction, research, training and mobilization, and you can buy resources, a country color or a premium account with it.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 330 MB of disk space and an internet connection for online play. The installer is 103 MB and doesn't need admin rights.

Where can I get help or report a bug?

On our Discord server. That's also where we post news about the game and new worlds.
